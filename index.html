How to play

The golem king has taken the old rune stones from the ruins of Valhalla. Fight your way through his stone army, gather the runes and prove yourself worthy of the halls of the gods.

Controls

← AWalk left → DWalk right ↑ WJump SpaceSword attack (costs stamina) MSound on / off

Goal

  • Defeat the golem king at the end of the level to win.
  • Collect all 8 runes – some can only be reached by jumping.
  • Red potions restore health when you are injured.
  • Watch your green stamina bar – it refills when you don't attack.
